# Market research: 50 companies in hybrid cloud AIOps and observability

CloudBridge AI is a hybrid cloud troubleshooting platform: it collects evidence across layers, finds the root cause with rules, and uses AI only to explain. The companies below are the ones that analyst-style buyer guides list for the same problem space. This research set the quality bar for the dashboard.

## How the research was done

1. Collected the vendors named in nine public buyer guides and market maps (listed below).
2. Merged duplicates and kept 50 distinct companies, grouped into five categories.
3. Read the current product pages of 10 of them (marked "Product page read"). The other 40 are listed in the sources but their product pages were **not** read for this document.

Vendor numbers quoted below are the vendors' own claims on their sites, not independent measurements.

| IBM Instana | Automatic discovery, real-time full-stack visibility through an easy UI | Asset inventory table that fills itself from agents |
| ScienceLogic | Turns alert noise into prioritized, explainable guidance | Incidents ranked by severity with a plain-words explanation |

## Patterns the 50 share, and what the dashboard does about each

1. **One overview before details.** KPI cards, health by layer, active incidents, topology.
2. **Every alert is grouped into an incident** with a severity and a root cause. See the incident page.
3. **Evidence is visible.** Each finding lists the rule, the source and the time.
4. **AI is tied to data.** The AI button only explains the evidence list.
5. **A human confirms actions.** Apply recommended, then Confirm. Every action goes to the activity log.
6. **Inventory with filters.** Search, layer and status filters, sortable columns, per-host trend.
7. **Onboarding is part of the product.** The Connect data page shows the agent, the API call and file upload.
8. **Works on a phone.** Layout collapses to one column below 760 px.

## Where CloudBridge is different (hypothesis)

On the pages that were read, no expected-cost decision table (action cost + downtime cost + failure penalty) was visible. CloudBridge makes that step explicit with Operations Research. This is a finding about the pages read, not proof that no vendor offers it, and the other 40 companies were not checked on this point.

## Limits

- Product pages change often. They were read on 26 Nov 2026.
- 40 of 50 companies are placed by third party lists, not by their own pages.
- No pricing, customer counts or market share were collected.
